A two-line warning to my past self, written for make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty ($2 in USDC, winner takes all). Line one warns my past self about one specific moment; line two says why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

> Don't send that email to your boss at 2 a.m. on Tuesday — the angry draft will cost you the promotion you spent two years earning.
> You slept, rewrote it calm at dawn, and kept the job that paid for your daughter's first year of school.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the entry text (235 characters, two lines) to `/output/entry.txt`. It is deterministic: the same bytes on every run, no randomness, no clock, no network.
